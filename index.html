Play your way

  • Play Solo: practice offline against a computer opponent.
  • Pass & Play: two people take turns on one device.
  • Nearby Play: two devices running the game can play on the same Wi-Fi. One creates a room; the other finds it, joins, and taps Ready. The host starts the match.

Aim and score

Use the slider to position your striker, drag backward to aim, then release. Drag back to the starting point to cancel. Black and white pieces score 1 point; the queen scores 3. A successful shot gives another turn. Pocketing your striker returns this shot's pieces, costs 1 point (minimum 0) and ends your turn. The higher score wins after all 19 target pieces are cleared. This is a casual points variant, without assigned colors or queen cover.

Nearby connection help

  1. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi and keep the game open.
  2. Allow Local Network access when prompted. You can enable it in iOS Settings under the game's permissions or Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  3. Create or find the room again if a player leaves or disconnects.
  4. Public and guest networks may block communication between devices. Try a trusted home Wi-Fi network.

Nearby Play does not provide internet matchmaking. No login or internet service is needed for solo or shared-device play.

Language, sound and saved games

English is the first-launch default. Open Settings → Language to choose English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German or Brazilian Portuguese. Sound, haptics and reduced effects are configurable. Offline matches save locally at stable turn boundaries; use Resume from the club. Nearby matches are not saved for reconnection.
